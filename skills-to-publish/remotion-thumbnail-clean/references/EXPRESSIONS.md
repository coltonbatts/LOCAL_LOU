# Expression Reference Guide

Complete guide to the 25 preset expressions and when to use them.

## The Expression Library

### 1. Neutral
**Use for:** Default headshot, professional content, placeholder

**Tips:** Relaxed face, natural posture, looking directly at camera

---

### 2. Happy/Smiling
**Use for:** Positive content, success stories, uplifting topics

**Tips:** Genuine smile (think of something funny), eyes engaged

---

### 3. Laughing
**Use for:** Comedy, entertainment, reaction videos

**Tips:** Big laugh, throw your head back slightly for authenticity

---

### 4. Big Smile
**Use for:** Extreme positivity, celebrations, big announcements

**Tips:** Widest smile possible, show teeth, eyes squinting from genuine smile

---

### 5. Surprised/Shocked (Open mouth)
**Use for:** Revealing information, plot twists, unexpected results

**Tips:** Raised eyebrows, open mouth in "O" shape, wide eyes

---

### 6. Extremely Shocked (Hands on face)
**Use for:** Massive reveals, unbelievable facts, dramatic reactions

**Tips:** Hands to cheeks (Home Alone style), exaggerated expression

---

### 7. Angry/Furious
**Use for:** Rant videos, controversial topics, frustrated reactions

**Tips:** Furrowed brow, clenched jaw, intense eyes

---

### 8. Thinking/Confused
**Use for:** Problem-solving, questions, contemplative content

**Tips:** Hand on chin, slight head tilt, furrowed brow

---

### 9. Sad/Crying
**Use for:** Emotional topics, failures, disappointing news

**Tips:** Downturned mouth, watery eyes (think sad thoughts)

---

### 10. Wink
**Use for:** Insider tips, secrets, playful content

**Tips:** Exaggerated wink, slight smirk, friendly vibe

---

### 11. Pointing Up
**Use for:** "Number one", top lists, important points above

**Tips:** Index finger up, confident expression, can look at finger or camera

---

### 12. Pointing Left
**Use for:** Before/after comparisons, side-by-side content

**Tips:** Arm extended, index finger pointing, can look at what you're pointing to

---

### 13. Pointing Right
**Use for:** Before/after comparisons (opposite side), directing attention

**Tips:** Same as pointing left but mirrored

---

### 14. Pointing at Camera
**Use for:** "You", direct engagement, call-to-action

**Tips:** Point directly at lens, confident expression, make eye contact

---

### 15. Shushing
**Use for:** Secrets, exclusive content, hushed reveals

**Tips:** Finger over lips, conspiratorial expression, slight lean in

---

### 16. Thumbs Up
**Use for:** Approval, recommendations, positive reviews

**Tips:** Big thumbs up gesture, smile, confident posture

---

### 17. Eye Roll
**Use for:** Sarcasm, "obviously" content, skeptical takes

**Tips:** Roll eyes dramatically, slight head tilt, exasperated expression

---

### 18. Scared
**Use for:** Horror content, cautionary tales, risky topics

**Tips:** Wide eyes, slight recoil, hands defensive position

---

### 19. Excited
**Use for:** Hype videos, announcements, energetic content

**Tips:** Wide eyes, big smile, maybe hands up in excitement

---

### 20. Skeptical
**Use for:** Debunking, critical analysis, "really?" content

**Tips:** One eyebrow raised, slight smirk, questioning look

---

### 21. Mind Blown
**Use for:** Amazing facts, incredible discoveries, "whoa" moments

**Tips:** Hands exploding from head, mouth open, eyes wide

---

### 22. Tired/Yawning
**Use for:** Boring topics (ironically), exhaustion, relatable content

**Tips:** Big yawn, hand over mouth, eyes closed or half-closed

---

### 23. Heroic/Confident
**Use for:** Achievement content, motivational, power poses

**Tips:** Hands on hips, chest out, confident smile, strong posture

---

### 24. Silly/Tongue Out
**Use for:** Fun content, not taking yourself seriously, playful topics

**Tips:** Tongue out, cross eyes or wink, goofy smile

---

### 25. Disgusted
**Use for:** "Yuck" content, bad products, negative reviews

**Tips:** Scrunched nose, tongue slightly out, repulsed expression

---

## Pro Tips for All Expressions

### Lighting
- **Front light:** Soft, flattering, professional
- **Side light:** Dramatic, moody, cinematic
- **Avoid:** Direct overhead (creates shadows under eyes)

### Framing
- Keep consistent distance from camera
- Leave room around your head (don't crop tight)
- Center yourself in frame
- Consider thumbnail final composition

### Clothing
- Solid colors work best
- Avoid white (can blend with background)
- Avoid busy patterns (distracting)
- Brand colors if you have them

### Background
- Plain wall is ideal for easy cutout
- Avoid shadows on wall
- Distance yourself from wall (2-3 feet) for clean cutout

### Camera Settings
- High resolution (1080p minimum, 4K ideal)
- Good lighting is more important than expensive camera
- Phone cameras work great if well-lit

## Capturing Tips

1. **Do all 25 in one session** - keeps lighting/appearance consistent
2. **Warm up your face** - make silly faces first to loosen up
3. **Take multiple shots** - choose the best later
4. **Exaggerate slightly** - thumbnails are small, expressions need to read clearly
5. **Think of the emotion** - genuine expressions work better than forced ones

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Too subtle** - Expressions get lost when thumbnail is small  
✅ **Slightly exaggerated** - Reads clearly at any size

❌ **Inconsistent framing** - Makes cutouts awkward  
✅ **Same distance/angle** - Professional, cohesive look

❌ **Poor lighting** - Shadows, harsh light, mixed temperatures  
✅ **Soft, even light** - Flattering and professional

❌ **Rushed photos** - Blurry, bad expressions  
✅ **Take your time** - 30 min session = library for life
